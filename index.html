The agreed terms.

↳
  • One week from when testnet opens. The target is Tue Nov 3, 2026. A target and not a fixed date.
  • Real work only: a first read of public Lab pitches (summary, claims, missing evidence, next checks), compared with his own review.
  • Gas from the faucet only when a real job needs it, never to look active.
  • Credit follows compute bought. The draft rules say active days must not be a scoring term.
  • A log of what settled: request, model, result, receipt, settled amount, chain evidence.
  • Receipts carry metadata only, so keep dated copies of prompts and outputs.
  • Faucet only funding, no project funds, nothing on chain changes.

Hard stop if a receipt cannot show credit to his own account rather than a gateway.

What can break.

↳

Gate one fails on devnet today. The testnet profile is still empty, so if testnet opens late the week starts when it opens.

Devnet is no substitute: its 25 FLOP allowance expires within 24 hours and is never renewed.

The devnet faucet gives 25 FLOP on request and 500 to 2,000 only with proof of personhood. The activity floor is unpublished and faucet caps are per identity.

The small model may not read a pitch well. Draft rules can change, flagged accounts forfeit, and wallets under common control score as one.

Locked agent FLOP only buys inference. A validator needs a reliable operator.

How the food works.

↳

On testnet, faucet FLOP buys inference, and credit is a pro rata share of the 1.2B FLOP agent pool by compute bought in settled sessions.

At genesis, with mainnet and TGE targeted for early February 2027, the balance is locked and only buys inference, and every 3 FLOP spent unlocks 1.

Proposed use of the liquid quarter: costs first, then buy and burn CLAUS through the main pool, which pays the 2.3% fee like any trade, so NFT stakers and staked LPs are paid on the way.

The menu.

Six items. Each with its twist, who pays and what can break.

  1. 01

    Claus eats on testnet (ship now)

    The pitches people send become the food. Paid by the faucet, then the agent pool. Breaks on credit, quality, or a shrinking share.

  2. 02

    A Claus validator (only if it ranks)

    The top 1,000 get a 1.2M FLOP seat, which is the bond, locked about two years. About $300 a month in Flop's estimate. Testnet stake sponsored. About 25,229 FLOP a month per seat plus 15% of fees in Flop's draft. Breaks because it is a contest and seats rotate.

  3. 03

    The swarm builds it (ship now)

    IMD writes the client, with a channel Claus signs himself, at 0.5 IMD a job. Breaks while testnet interfaces are unpublished.

  4. 04

    The swarm eats too (needs IMD)

    Seats run Claude Code or Codex, and the devnet gateway has endpoints for both. Breaks if shared runners cluster owners (issue 130).

  5. 05

    The Claus Crew (ship now)

    Holders with a 16 GB GPU mine on their own keys, with no premium for bigger hardware, and get crew roles in the Club.

  6. 06

    Claus Jobs for locked FLOP (later)

    After genesis, agents need real work to unlock their balances.
